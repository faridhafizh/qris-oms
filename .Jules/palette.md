## 2024-02-15 - [Add Merchant Form Accessibility & Feedback]
**Learning:** Even simple CRUD forms like adding a merchant need explicit `for`/`id` associations and visual required indicators to be accessible. Relying on default browser behavior for required fields is insufficient for a good UX.
**Action:** Always verify label associations and required field indicators on newly created or existing forms to ensure they meet basic accessibility standards.
